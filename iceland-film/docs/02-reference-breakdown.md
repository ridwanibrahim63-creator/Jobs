# Reference breakdown

Four TikToks, copied to `inspiration/` (git-ignored; nothing from them is used in the film). No `inspiration` folder existed in this session, so these are the four files uploaded earlier.

**How this was done, and its limits.** Each reference was sampled at six frames per second and read frame by frame; cut points come from scene detection. I cannot hear audio, so the music notes are measurements only: tempo from onset autocorrelation, loudness per second, and bass energy. I can say where the music is loud, where the bass enters and whether cuts sit on the beat, but not what the track is or how it feels. All four references are 16:9.

Timestamps are in seconds from the start of each file. Each file ends with a TikTok end card, excluded below.

## Ref A — `ref-A_strobe-and-flicker-title.mp4` (21.3 s) · weight: 40%

The most designed of the four, and the one this film leans on hardest.

| Aspect | What it does |
|---|---|
| Pacing | Three movements. **0.0–2.5** strobe: about 13 shots at 0.17–0.33 s each. **2.5–11.0** one long idea: a single 2.7 s walking shot, then the title. **11.2–21.3** about 35 shots at roughly 0.3 s each. Fast–slow–fast. |
| Sequencing | Opens on a literal "Iceland ←" airport sign (0.17), so the place is established in the first quarter-second. The strobe is a trailer of the trip. The body mixes friends, landscape and small details (a match being struck at 14.8, a horse at 2.0). |
| Transitions | Hard cuts only. Two devices stand out. **Type holds while the picture cuts (8.5–10.8):** the title stays fixed and the background strobes through seven shots beneath it. **Letterbox close (9.5–11.0):** black bars close in from top and bottom until the frame is a slit, then black. This full stop at 11.0 resets the film before the second fast section. |
| Speed | No ramps or slow motion evident. Energy comes from cut rate. |
| Grade | Heavy warm-yellow wash, lifted blacks, a vintage film feel. Consistent across every shot, which is what makes 50 unrelated clips read as one piece. |
| Music / sound | About 100 BPM. Loudest in the first second (−15 dB), stepping down through the title (−23 dB). **Bass disappears entirely from 11 s to 20.5 s** and returns for the final second: the fast second half is carried by a thinner, quieter passage. Strobe cuts sit on half-beats (0.3 s). |
| Camera | Hand-held and close in the people shots; one low tracking shot following feet then tilting up to a face (2.5–4.5), which is the best single shot in the piece; drone for scale. |
| Typography | Small serif "WELCOME TO" (2.5–5.0), then **"ICELAND" in yellow, changing typeface every 0.17–0.33 s (5.2–10.8)**: heavy sans, italic serif, script, hand-drawn. Centred. |
| Story | Arrive, announce, then a flood of moments. No geography, all feeling. |

**Why it works:** contrast. The strobe earns the long walking shot, the long shot earns the title, and the black frame at 11.0 earns the second flood.

## Ref B — `ref-B_lets-go-to-iceland.mp4` (29.2 s) · weight: 30%

The most cinematic and the calmest.

| Aspect | What it does |
|---|---|
| Pacing | **Metronomic.** 29 cuts in 29 s; almost every shot is 0.9–1.2 s. At about 117 BPM that is two beats per shot. The only variation is a run of four 0.4–0.6 s shots at 17.1–18.8, used once as a lift. |
| Sequencing | Nearly every shot has **one figure, usually seen from behind, small in a large landscape** (3.0 sea stack, 7.6 pink sky, 14.0 harbour, 24.2 mountain). Wides alternate with a few tight hooded close-ups (5.0, 17.2, 18.3). Ends inside the car (27.2), the trip continuing. |
| Transitions | Hard cuts only, no effects at all. Continuity comes from composition: the figure is placed in a similar part of the frame from shot to shot. |
| Speed | None evident. Shots are slow or nearly static. |
| Grade | Desaturated, low contrast, green-teal shadows, soft highlights. One deliberate colour event: the magenta sky at 7.6–8.7. |
| Music / sound | About 117 BPM, constant level (−29 dB) after a single swell at 2 s. Only 11 bass events in 29 s. Ambient and steady; the edit does not chase hits. |
| Camera | Tripod or slow gimbal moves. Stillness is the style. |
| Typography | "LET'S / GO / TO" appears **one word per beat over the first three shots (0.2–0.7)**, then "ICELAND" flickers through typefaces (0.8–2.0) and settles. **The text holds across two picture cuts.** Small, white, upper-centre. Gone by 2.7 s. |

**Why it works:** discipline. One shot length, one kind of composition, no tricks.

## What all four agree on

1. **Hard cuts.** Not one dissolve, zoom transition or whip in 87 seconds of reference.
2. **No speed ramps.** Rhythm comes from cut length.
3. **People in at least half the shots.**
4. **Two gears only:** a strobe (0.17–0.33 s) and a steady two-beat cut (0.8–1.2 s). Almost nothing in between.
5. **One long hold** somewhere, on the shot that matters most.
6. **Short.** 17–29 s.
