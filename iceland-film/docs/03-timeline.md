## Sound map

Riser under the map and flashes with one hit per flash → impact on the title shot (2.4) → pulse and piano motif through the trip, impacts at 4.8, 12.6 and 19.2 → riser from 21.1, everything drops out at 23.6 → biggest impact at 24.0 with full pad, drums and the motif an octave up → groove thins at 30.0 → soft impacts on the white-out (31.8) and the aurora (34.8) → 2.6 s tail to silence. Synthetic wind throughout; water under Seljalandsfoss and the Gullfoss shots.

## Transitions

Hard cuts on the beat throughout, plus: the flash montage, flash-accented cuts on the title and the drop, the in-camera white-out into the sunset, and a dip to black from sunset into the aurora (day into night).

## Photos

Six photos are used, each with keyframed movement (push-in or pan) so none sits static, and never more than two in a row.
