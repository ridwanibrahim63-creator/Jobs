# Story Bank — Master STAR+R Stories

This file accumulates your best interview stories over time. Each evaluation (Block F) adds new stories here. Instead of memorizing 100 answers, maintain 5-10 deep stories that you can bend to answer almost any behavioral question.

## How it works

1. Every time `/career-ops oferta` generates Block F (Interview Plan), new STAR+R stories get appended here
2. Before your next interview, review this file — your stories are already organized by theme
3. The "Big Three" questions can be answered with stories from this bank:
   - "Tell me about yourself" → combine 2-3 stories into a narrative
   - "Tell me about your most impactful project" → pick your highest-impact story
   - "Tell me about a conflict you resolved" → find a story with a Reflection

### [Regulatory Risk Analysis] CM Price Maker Investigation
**Source:** Report #001 — Abu Dhabi Energy Group — Senior Specialist, Carbon Policy & Markets
**S:** Ofgem needed to assess whether Capacity Market auction behaviour was genuinely competitive or strategically distorted
**T:** Lead an independent investigation into Price Maker Memorandum submissions, assess market impact, advise on regulatory response
**A:** Designed and ran quantitative and qualitative investigation; synthesised findings for senior decision-making
**R:** Produced findings that directly informed Ofgem's regulatory position and strategic response
**Reflection:** Market behaviour signals require cross-market context to interpret correctly — isolated analysis can miss the bigger picture
**Best for questions about:** Analytical rigour, regulatory investigation, independent judgement, working under scrutiny

### [Regulatory Framework Maintenance] Capacity Market Rules Governance
**Source:** Report #001 — Abu Dhabi Energy Group — Senior Specialist, Carbon Policy & Markets
**S:** CM Rules needed to evolve as legislation and market conditions changed rapidly; investor confidence was at risk
**T:** Ensure Rules remained investable and legally sound while decarbonisation policy shifted the landscape around them

### [Complex Multi-Stakeholder Alignment] Balancing Services Reform Engagement
**Source:** Report #001 — Abu Dhabi Energy Group — Senior Specialist, Carbon Policy & Markets
**S:** Long-term balancing services reform at NESO required buy-in from commercially opposed parties — generators, flexibility providers, investors, aggregators, government
**T:** Build consensus across parties with conflicting commercial interests while maintaining reform momentum
**A:** Structured engagement programme; presented strategic options; absorbed commercial feedback; iterated proposals
**R:** Reforms progressed with broad industry support; commercially credible roadmap delivered
**Reflection:** The most effective engagement frames proposals as "here's the problem we share" not "here's our answer" — positions change when parties feel heard
**Best for questions about:** Stakeholder engagement, influencing without authority, complex reform, conflict resolution, communication

### [Proactive Risk Management] System Transformation Risk Assessment
**Source:** Report #001 — Abu Dhabi Energy Group — Senior Specialist, Carbon Policy & Markets
**S:** Emerging legislation and decarbonisation policy at NESO created overlapping, sometimes contradictory signals for the market
